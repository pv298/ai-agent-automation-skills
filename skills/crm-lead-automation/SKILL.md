# CRM and Lead Automation

## Workflow
1. Map lead sources, consent language, fields, lifecycle stages and business ownership.
2. Define required fields and validation rules at capture.
3. Normalize phone, email, location, source and campaign fields without guessing missing values.
4. Deduplicate using approved identifiers and documented matching rules.
5. Enrich only from authorized sources and record provenance.
6. Score or route leads using transparent, business-relevant criteria.
7. Assign owner, SLA, status and next action.
8. Trigger only consent-appropriate follow-ups with suppression and unsubscribe checks.
9. Sync lifecycle outcomes back to source systems where appropriate.
10. Monitor duplicates, invalid records, response time, qualification and conversion.

## Deliverables
Lifecycle map, data dictionary, deduplication rules, routing matrix, consent policy, automation design and KPI plan.

## Guardrails
Do not scrape or purchase personal contact lists. Do not infer sensitive traits. Restrict access to lead data, honor deletion and opt-out requests, and avoid exposing customer details in logs or test fixtures. Obtain approval before activating outbound messages.
